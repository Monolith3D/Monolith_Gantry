## Changelog

### 04.11.2026

- round bearing seats in the printed XY joints, motorless mounts, rear mounts and double-shear housings
- simplified printed rear mounts: counterbored stepper screws, fourth double-shear housing screw removed with its nut and washer
- VT NP spacers replace the indexing ribs on the printed VT mounts (spacer STLs added)
- sheet metal V2 mount plates take the standard 40 x 9 mm belt clamp, which replaces the hybrid Z-belt clips (now in STLs/Legacy)
- updated printed Z joint NP spacer
- per-station files (Front_left, Front_right, Rear_left, Rear_right, XY_left, XY_right, Z_joint), like the LDO kit
- Frames files no longer repeat the T-nuts and Y carriages that the station files now hold
- DXFs flattened to exact Z=0 for strict importers and nesting tools
- section BOM CSVs removed: the Monolith Builder makes the BOM for your exact build (the V2 and VT reusable-parts lists stay)

### 15.06.2025

- 695 W5 bearing support
- minor visual changes
- S45R long-shafted steppers in CAD
- subassembly fixes

### 10.06.2025

- sheet metal tensioner wire delete
- gantry-mounted X-endstop option

### 28.01.2025

- fixed subassembly origins
- fixed some naming oopsies
- ditched the printed alignment helpers for the sheet metal version
- more corner reliefs on motor mount plates
- smaller assembly/BOM fixes
