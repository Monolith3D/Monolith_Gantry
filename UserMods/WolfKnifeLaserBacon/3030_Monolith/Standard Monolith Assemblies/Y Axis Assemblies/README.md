# Y-Axis & Rear Motor Assemblies

STEP files for the 3030 Monolith Y-axis, frame extrusion, and rear motor assemblies.

## Files

- `3030 Complete Y Assembly.step`  
  Complete Y-axis assembly.

- `3030 Extrusions.step`  
  3030 extrusion layout used for the gantry assembly.

- `3030 Rear Left Motor Assembly.step`  
  Left-side rear motor assembly.

- `3030 Rear Motor Assemblies.step`  
  Complete rear motor assembly containing both left and right sides.

- `3030 Rear Right Motor Assembly.step`  
  Right-side rear motor assembly.

## Notes

These files are intended for the 3030 Monolith gantry configuration.

The complete Y assembly can be used to verify overall fitment, rail placement, motor positioning, and component relationships.

Left and right rear motor assemblies are also provided separately for easier reference, modification, and integration into larger CAD assemblies.
